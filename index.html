01 / The proposition

From dependency
to cooperation.

This framework does not attempt to commercialize affection. It separates economic contribution, household production, care and personal financial autonomy so a family can discuss money without reducing people to money.

01 · VISIBILITY

See the work

Cooking, cleaning, childcare, planning, procurement, care and household administration become visible economic contributions.

02 · RECOGNITION

Value the work

Use replacement-cost references as a measurement tool, while remembering that household value is not automatically cash income.

03 · AUTONOMY

Protect freedom

Give adults defined personal financial space so domestic contribution does not automatically mean financial dependence.

05 / The charter

Six movements
toward equity.

A family can adopt the framework gradually. It is a conversation and accounting practice, not a compulsory contract.

01VISIBILITY

List the work that keeps the household alive.

02RECOGNITION

Estimate replacement value without pretending it is new cash.

03ACCOUNTING

Separate family money from personal money.

04COMPENSATION

Agree an affordable domestic work dividend.

05AUTONOMY

Give adults defined personal financial space.

The cooperative principle

“Income is contribution—not ownership of the family.”

The objective is a household that can recognize work, distribute responsibility, maintain solvency, protect individual dignity and allow people of different ages and circumstances to contribute according to capacity.
